# Topics and lenses

Identifiers stay attached to the same topic or lens across blocks. Add new identifiers after the existing ones.

## Research and experiment records

R-XX identifies one complete research-gap-dossier run. Record its T-XX topic and selected L-XX lenses with the dossier. Repeating an investigation with the same topic and lenses can produce a new R record.

E-XX identifies one experiment. For an experiment arising from a dossier, record its originating R record and candidate. An R record can lead to no experiments, one experiment, or multiple experiments. Repeated executions of the same experiment are runs within its E record.

Existing R01, R03, E00, E01, and E02 identifiers retain their current names. This convention does not rename existing files or change their tooling.
