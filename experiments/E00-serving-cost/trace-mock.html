1. One request, start to finish (seconds)

Prefill reads the whole prompt once, then decode makes one token per step. 128 tokens of output. The 8k prompt costs 10x the prefill, but each decode step only gets 1.5x slower.

2. One decode step (milliseconds)

Zoomed in on a single token. The GPU walks through 36 identical layers, then the output layer (lm_head). The dashed line is the floor: the time just to stream 2.26 GB of weights at the best measured bandwidth (133 GB/s). The "by kind" row is the same time regrouped by colour, not the real order.

3. Inside one layer (milliseconds)

The real order of work in one layer. Matmul times are measured. The purple "small kernel" bars were measured only as one total for the whole step (5.1 ms), so here that total is spread evenly for illustration. Only attention changes between 1k and 8k.
